# Fabio Maffioletti — CV Site

A personal curriculum vitae and portfolio site for Fabio Maffioletti, published on the web and linked from professional profiles.

## Language

**Profile**:
The identity, role, and contact details that describe the site's owner.
_Avoid_: User, account, person record

**Curriculum**:
The complete professional record published on the site, covering experience, education, skills, and links.
_Avoid_: Resume data, CV JSON, content blob

**Company**:
An organization where the owner has worked, containing one or more roles.
_Avoid_: Employer, job, position

**Role**:
A position held at a company, with a date range and a list of achievements.
_Avoid_: Job, title, stint

**Section**:
A titled block of the curriculum, such as summary, professional experience, or education.
_Avoid_: Panel, widget, card

**Privacy page**:
The standalone MissPlitty privacy policy, published outside the main curriculum and referenced by external stores.
_Avoid_: Policy route, legal page, terms
